## What the app does

The current build offers a weather panel, clock, shortcuts into installed camera/timer/music apps, a short audio test, and an experimental local video-only recorder with visible controls. The Hermes panel, EQ controls, remote commands, and wake-word features are not implemented. Features can fail or change while this prototype develops.
