## What the app does

The current build offers a weather panel with current conditions, an animated detail view and a live seven-day forecast, clock, a foreground on-tablet camera preview, shortcuts into installed photo/timer/music apps, short beep and spoken-reply tests, **Speak** push-to-talk and optional continuously listening local Vosk recognition, and an optional Android Home-app role. Hands-free must be explicitly enabled; it requests microphone permission then runs as a foreground service with a notification Stop action. On Android 13+ it also needs notification permission, and it stops or will not start if app notifications or the microphone notification channel are blocked. The enabled choice persists for eligible Android foreground-service restarts and can resume when Station becomes visible, including after Home is opened on startup. **It does not install a boot receiver or guarantee uninterrupted capture across reboot, system restrictions, or a microphone failure.** Stop in-app or through the notification to clear the saved choice. While it runs, the tablet microphone captures audio even when the screen is off or another app is visible. It recognizes “Hey Station” or “Station” command prefixes locally; “Hey Station” alone gets an acknowledgment and opens a fixed 45-second window for multiple different bounded follow-up commands without repeating the prefix. Another wake phrase resets the window. After a wake, an unhandled final transcript may appear briefly on the Station screen but is not saved. This is not a guaranteed dedicated wake-word engine. Time replies work outside the foreground app; weather requests expand the Weather panel when Station is visible, and background requests attempt to open it or provide a tap-to-open notification if Android blocks background activity launch. Play/pause/next media keys can be sent to Android (without guaranteeing playback); other commands ask you to open Station. There is no in-app audio/video recording or remote camera feed. The Hermes panel, EQ controls, and remote commands are not implemented. Fifteen bounded actions have 100 documented short phrase variants, not 100 distinct capabilities. A separately installed offline English Piper/Kristin model engine can be chosen in Studio Audio and switched back to the system voice; it is not bundled with Station. If unavailable, Station reports an error instead of silently substituting the system voice. Features can fail or change while this prototype develops; in-room recognition of every phrase and the model voice's subjective quality are not guaranteed.

## Your responsibilities

You control installation, Android's reversible choice of Home app, and device permissions. Enable continuous listening only where ambient microphone capture is appropriate and you have the necessary consent; use the live camera and speech recognition with respect for others' privacy. Stop hands-free with its in-app control or notification, or revoke microphone permission in Android settings. Continuous capture uses battery, may be interrupted by Android, and may mishear commands or activate falsely. The local Speak microphone is on only after tapping Speak, stops after one command or nine seconds, and can be cancelled with the same control. Both modes use the bundled offline model; there is no separate external recognizer. You still control the selected TTS engine and external photo/music apps. Restore your previous launcher through Android Home-app settings if Station is unsuitable. Keep the tablet physically secure. This prototype is not a monitored security system, emergency alert service, or record of an event.
