## What the app does

The current build offers a weather panel, clock, a foreground on-tablet camera preview, shortcuts into installed photo/timer/music apps, and a short audio test. There is no in-app recording or remote camera feed. The Hermes panel, EQ controls, remote commands, and wake-word features are not implemented. Features can fail or change while this prototype develops.
