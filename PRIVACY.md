# Privacy Policy — TCL Station

Effective: October 9, 2026

**Scope.** This policy covers the open-source TCL Station Android app in this repository, maintained under the GitHub account **Aero7Code**. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, or TCL Station cloud. This describes the code currently published, not future integrations or unrelated apps launched from the dashboard.

## Information used by the app

- **Weather:** The app sends configured latitude/longitude to the Open-Meteo forecast API over HTTPS when weather loads or you refresh it. The default repository configuration uses an example city-center point; a builder can provide a private local override. The app does not request GPS/device location permission. Open-Meteo receives the request and can see its network source (including an IP address); its own terms/privacy policy explains its handling of API requests.[1] We do not operate that service.
- **Live camera:** Android camera permission is requested when you tap **Open live camera**. The app displays front or back camera frames on the tablet screen while the preview is open. It does not record, save, transmit, or analyze these frames; the preview closes when you leave the app. Android and system-level components may have their own camera-access indicators and behavior.
- **Push-to-talk:** Tapping **Speak** hands microphone capture and recognition to an installed Android recognizer (on the tested tablet, Google's speech app). That recognizer may send speech and transcripts to its provider according to its settings and policies; offline processing is **not guaranteed**. Station receives returned text in memory and matches its bounded commands locally. Speak is separate from the optional continuous listener and pauses it while the external recognizer is active.
- **Optional continuous local listening:** Only when you enable **Hands-free** does Station request Android microphone permission and start its foreground microphone service with an ongoing notification and **Stop listening** action. Tap the in-app control or notification action to stop it. While enabled and running, the **tablet microphone captures ambient audio continuously, including while the screen is off or other apps are visible**. A bundled Vosk model recognizes speech locally/offline. A final phrase beginning with “Hey Station” or “Station” can trigger a bounded command. A recognized “Hey Station” alone also triggers a spoken acknowledgment and permits one bounded follow-up command without a prefix for up to 35 seconds. This is not a separate guaranteed wake-word detector, and misrecognition or false activations are possible. Recognition pauses for Speak and spoken replies. The listener does not start at boot, and it stops if its service is terminated. Station does not save microphone audio or transcripts to files, send them to a TCL Station server, or send them to Vosk for cloud recognition. The Echo Studio aux connection is tablet **output**, not a microphone input.
- **Speech output and background commands:** With hands-free running, time and weather answers may be spoken when Station is not in the foreground; weather uses the last summary saved in app-private preferences and can be stale. Other commands run only while Station is foreground; otherwise it asks you to open Station. Text for spoken responses is handed to the installed Android text-to-speech engine, whose provider may process it under its own policy. Station does not keep speech transcripts.
- **Home app:** If you choose Station as the Android default Home screen, Android stores your launcher choice. This does not grant Station device-owner/kiosk access, enable a boot receiver, or disable Android Settings. Page 3 opens the system Home-app settings so you can select another launcher.
- **Other actions:** **Take photo** launches another installed camera app; photos are controlled by that app, not TCL Station. Timer and alarm buttons launch Android's clock handler. **Open YouTube Music** hands off to an installed app or browser; accounts, cookies, and playback on those services are governed by their providers' policies. The short test beep and spoken-reply test do not record audio; the spoken test sends its text to the installed Android TTS engine as described above.
- **Local status:** No camera status, identifiers, audio, or media are sent to a TCL Station server. Android, the device manufacturer, and other apps may process data according to their own settings and policies.

## Storage and deletion

The current build does not create camera or microphone recordings or store speech transcripts. It caches the last weather summary (for background spoken weather answers) and your original/alternate voice choice in app-private preferences, and unpacks its bundled offline speech model into the app-specific external-files directory (`Android/data/com.aero.tclstation/files`), not the internal private preferences directory. On startup after an upgrade, it attempts to delete only the old recorder's matching `station-*.mp4` and `.partial` clips in its former app-private `security-recordings` directory; unrelated files are left alone. If cleanup cannot complete, clear TCL Station's app storage or uninstall it to remove the app's data, including the cached weather summary and unpacked model. The app disables standard Android backup, but independently created copies or system-level backups are outside its control. Photos created by a *different* camera app must be managed through that app or Android storage. Do not share photos or detailed location configuration in public GitHub issues.

## Sharing and changes

TCL Station does not sell personal data. Its weather request goes to Open-Meteo; optional hands-free recognition runs locally with bundled Vosk, whereas the separate Speak recognizer and synthesized voice use Android providers; tapping external shortcuts may send you to independent providers. A future host, Hermes, sync, or cloud feature would require a policy and code update before being described as available. Changes to this policy will be published in the repository. For non-sensitive privacy questions, use GitHub issues under **Aero7Code/tcl-station**; do not post personal information there.
