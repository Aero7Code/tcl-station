# Privacy Policy — TCL Station

Effective: October 9, 2026

**Scope.** This policy covers the open-source TCL Station Android app in this repository, maintained under the GitHub account **Aero7Code**. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, or TCL Station cloud. This describes the code currently published, not future integrations or unrelated apps launched from the dashboard.

## Information used by the app

- **Weather:** The app sends configured latitude/longitude to the Open-Meteo forecast API over HTTPS when weather loads or you refresh it. The default repository configuration uses an example city-center point; a builder can provide a private local override. The app does not request GPS/device location permission. Open-Meteo receives the request and can see its network source (including an IP address); its own terms/privacy policy explains its handling of API requests.[1] We do not operate that service.
- **Live camera:** Android camera permission is requested when you tap **Open live camera**. The app displays front or back camera frames on the tablet screen while the preview is open. It does not record, save, transmit, or analyze these frames; the preview closes when you leave the app. Android and system-level components may have their own camera-access indicators and behavior.
- **Push-to-talk:** Tapping **Speak** requests Android microphone permission (if needed), pauses the optional continuous listener, and recognizes one command locally using the bundled Vosk model. The microphone stops after a recognized result, a nine-second timeout, cancellation, or leaving Station. The camera preview closes before microphone capture. Station does not send microphone audio or transcripts to a recognition provider, store them in files, or use Google's separate recognizer for Speak.
- **Optional persistent local listening:** Only when you enable **Hands-free** does Station request Android microphone permission and start its foreground microphone service with an ongoing notification and **Stop listening** action. On Android 13+, Station also requests notification permission; if app notifications or its microphone channel are blocked, the listener will not start or stops within its next visibility check so a hidden Stop notification cannot coexist with ongoing capture. The on/off choice is stored in app-private preferences. Once enabled, Station asks Android to restart the foreground service after an eligible system process restart and resumes it when Station next becomes visible (including if it is launched as the Home app). It does **not** install a boot receiver or claim that Android can guarantee microphone capture through a reboot or service kill; if Station is not launched after boot, it may remain off. The service stops and clears the saved choice on a microphone/model/speech safety failure; tap to re-enable after resolving it. Tap the in-app control or notification action to turn it off persistently. While enabled and running, the **tablet microphone captures ambient audio continuously, including while the screen is off or other apps are visible**. A bundled Vosk model recognizes speech locally/offline. A final phrase beginning with “Hey Station” or “Station” can trigger a bounded command. A recognized “Hey Station” alone triggers a spoken acknowledgment and opens a fixed **45-second** window for multiple different follow-up commands without a prefix; another wake phrase resets that window. Repeating an identical phrase within five seconds is suppressed. This is not a separate guaranteed wake-word detector, and misrecognition or false activations are possible. After a wake, Station may briefly display an unhandled final transcript on-screen to help you diagnose what Vosk heard; it is not saved or added to the notification. Recognition pauses for Speak and spoken replies. Station does not save microphone audio or transcripts to files, send them to a TCL Station server, or send them to Vosk for cloud recognition. The Echo Studio aux connection is tablet **output**, not a microphone input.
- **Weather, speech output and background commands:** Station sends its configured area coordinates to Open-Meteo for *current conditions and a seven-day daily forecast*; detailed Weather displays data from the latest response and a condition-matched animated scene when available, not a saved weather history. A weather voice command expands the Weather panel when Station is visible. In the background, Station tries to bring its Weather panel forward and posts a tap-to-open notification if Android restricts that transition. The previous app-private weather-summary cache is deleted on next launch; it is not used to invent a current scene or weekly forecast. Background time answers may be spoken. Background play requests send an Android media key even when playback is paused; pause/next require active music playback. A key dispatch does not establish that a player acted on it; other commands ask you to open Station. Spoken-response text is passed to the selected Android text-to-speech engine. By default this is the system engine, whose provider may process text under its own policy. If you separately install and explicitly select the sherpa-onnx Piper/Kristin offline model engine, synthesis runs in that separate local Android app instead; no remote speech service is configured by Station for that choice. If it fails, Station reports the error rather than silently sending text to the system engine. Station does not keep speech transcripts.
- **Home app:** If you choose Station as the Android default Home screen, Android stores your launcher choice. Opening Station as Home after boot can resume your previously enabled microphone preference. This does not grant Station device-owner/kiosk access, enable a boot receiver, or disable Android Settings. Page 3 opens the system Home-app settings so you can select another launcher.
- **Other actions:** **Take photo** launches another installed camera app; photos are controlled by that app, not TCL Station. Timer and alarm buttons launch Android's clock handler. **Open YouTube Music** hands off to an installed app or browser; accounts, cookies, and playback on those services are governed by their providers' policies. The short test beep and spoken-reply test do not record audio; the spoken test sends its text to the installed Android TTS engine as described above.
- **Local status:** No camera status, identifiers, audio, or media are sent to a TCL Station server. Android, the device manufacturer, and other apps may process data according to their own settings and policies.

## Storage and deletion

The current build does not create camera or microphone recordings or store speech transcripts. It keeps a small, rotating Station diagnostics log in its app-specific external-files directory (`Android/data/com.aero.tclstation/files/diagnostics`): two files of at most 32 KiB each with timestamps, transient process IDs, and fixed lifecycle/speech-engine event names. It contains no recognized words, audio, coordinates, or spoken reply text; anyone with authorized USB debugging access to the tablet can retrieve it, and Station does not upload it. The old app-private weather-summary cache is removed when this build starts, and no current-weather summary or forecast is saved to preferences. The app keeps the hands-free on/off choice and system/model voice selection in app-private preferences, and unpacks its bundled Vosk recognition model into the app-specific external-files directory (`Android/data/com.aero.tclstation/files`), not the internal private preferences directory. The separately installed optional TTS model engine manages its **own** app data; uninstall it separately if you no longer want its voice model. On startup after an upgrade, Station attempts to delete only the old recorder's matching `station-*.mp4` and `.partial` clips in its former app-private `security-recordings` directory; unrelated files are left alone. If cleanup cannot complete, clear TCL Station's app storage or uninstall it to remove Station's data, including the recognition model and remaining app-private preferences. The app disables standard Android backup, but independently created copies or system-level backups are outside its control. Photos created by a *different* camera app must be managed through that app or Android storage. Do not share photos or detailed location configuration in public GitHub issues.

## Sharing and changes

TCL Station does not sell personal data. Its weather request goes to Open-Meteo; optional hands-free recognition and tap-to-talk both use the bundled local Vosk model. Speech output uses the system TTS provider unless you separately install and select the local sherpa-onnx model engine. Tapping external shortcuts may send you to independent providers. A future host, Hermes, sync, or cloud feature would require a policy and code update before being described as available. Changes to this policy will be published in the repository. For non-sensitive privacy questions, use GitHub issues under **Aero7Code/tcl-station**; do not post personal information there.
